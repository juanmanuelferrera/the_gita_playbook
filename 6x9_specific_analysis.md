## 🎯 WHY 6×9 SPECIFICALLY NEEDS INDENT

### Mathematical Reasoning:

6×9 inch page dimensions:
- Total width: 6 inches (152.4mm)
- Typical margins: Inner 0.75", Outer 0.55" = 1.3" total
- **Usable text width: 4.7 inches (119mm)**

4.7 inches is NARROW compared to:
- 8.5×11 paper: 6-7 inches text width
- Computer screen: 5-8 inches reading column

#### Problem with vertical spacing on 6×9:

```
Calculation for YOUR book:

Text width: 4.7 inches = 119mm
Average line length: 60-70 characters
Average paragraph: 4-6 lines

WITH INDENT:
- 6 lines × 0pt extra = 0pt wasted per paragraph
- 12 paragraphs per page × 0pt = 0pt wasted per page
- Vertical space used: 100% for content

WITH 4PT PARSKIP:
- 6 lines × 0pt + 4pt after = 4pt wasted per paragraph  
- 12 paragraphs per page × 4pt = 48pt wasted per page
- Usable page height: ~700pt
- Waste percentage: 48/700 = 6.9% per page

Result: You lose 6.9% of EVERY PAGE in 6×9 format!
```

### Visual Density on 6×9:

The smaller page size means every millimeter counts.

**6×9 with indent:** Looks professional, efficient, readable
**6×9 with parskip:** Looks sparse, amateurish, wasteful
